December 22, 2025

To Whom It May Concern,

The Terumo Life Science Foundation hereby confirms the awarding of a 2025 R&D Subsidy (Overseas) grant for the following research project:
Project title: “Early Detection of Alzheimer’s Disease and Mild Cognitive Impairment in Vietnamese Older Adults through Speech Analysis”
Project leader: Dr. Ha Thi Thanh Huong
International collaborator: MD., PhD. Taishiro Kishimoto (Keio University School of Medicine, Japan)
Project collaborators: MD. Hoang Tien Trong Nghia (175 Military Hospital, Vietnam), Dr. Ngo Thanh Hoan (Florida Southern College, USA)
Student researcher: Vu Nguyen Minh Duc (International University—Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam)

This letter confirms the following:
Approved Research Funding: The total approved grant amount is 2,000,000 JPY.
Detailed Budget Allocation: The budget was reviewed and approved as detailed in the budget table below.
Student Researcher Involvement: We confirm the participation of student researcher Vu Nguyen Minh Duc, including his involvement in a two-week training exchange to Japan to acquire advanced speech processing and analysis methodologies under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Taishiro Kishimoto at Keio University School of Medicine.
Official Development Assistance (ODA) Status: Importantly, the Terumo Life Science Foundation confirms that this funding does not constitute Official Development Assistance (ODA). It does not originate from governmental development-aid mechanisms, concessional finance, or bilateral/multilateral aid programs.
