Ho Chi Minh City, November 2nd, 2024
To Whom It May Concern,
Subject: Letter of Support for Dr. Ngo Thi Lua and Dr. Ha Thi Thanh Huong’s participation in EU-ASEAN Sustainable Connectivity Package - Higher Education Programme opportunity
I am writing on behalf of International University, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City to express our enthusiastic support for Dr. Ngo Thi Lua and Dr. Ha Thi Thanh Huong joining the project: " Intercontinental Capacity Building for Model Sharing and Digital Transformation of Neurodegenerative Disease Care". This project aligns with the objectives of the EU-ASEAN Sustainable Connectivity Package - Higher Education Programme (SCOPE-HE), fostering digital transformation and sustainable regional collaboration.

Our International University (IU) is a member of Vietnam National University, Ho Chi Minh City (VNU-HCMC), known for pioneering higher education that blends international standards with a strong Vietnamese cultural identity. IU's mission is to become one of the top research-oriented universities in Vietnam and Asia, providing high-quality education and fostering innovation. Our vision is to become one of the leading research – oriented universities in Vietnam and Asia, with an international, autonomous, and creative educational institution with and the aim of nurturing talent and providing high quality labor for both domestic and international workforces.

The initiative "Intercontinental Capacity Building for Model Sharing and Digital Transformation of Neurodegenerative Disease Care" developed by Professor Guy Nagels from Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB), Brussels, Belgium under the SCOPE-HE programme addresses the pressing global challenge posed by neurodegenerative diseases, such as Multiple Sclerosis (MS), Neuromyelitis Optica (NMO), and Alzheimer’s Disease (AD). The initiative aims to harness digital transformation in healthcare, integrating artificial intelligence (AI), big data, and telemedicine to enhance research, diagnostics, and treatment approaches. By fostering collaboration between European and Southeast Asian academic and research institutions, the project supports SCOPE-HE’s objectives of promoting higher education partnerships and sustainable capacity building.

I would like to endorse the participation of IU in the abovementioned SCOPE-HE initiative led by Prof. Guy Nagels and strongly support the leading participation of our academics - Dr. Ngo Thi Lua and Dr. Ha Thi Thanh Huong. With their extensive expertise and commitment to research and teaching, they will play key roles in developing teaching and training materials, facilitating collaborative research, and organizing workshops aligned with the project’s work packages.  Dr. Ngo Thi Lua and Dr. Ha Thi Thanh Huong’s involvement underscores IU’s dedication to advancing digital healthcare research and interdisciplinary education. As affirmed by Professor Guy Nagels, the management of IU has taken note of and supports the project application “Intercontinental Capacity Building for Model Sharing and Digital Transformation of Neurodegenerative Disease Care”.
In conclusion, the International University at Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City is excited about the project’s potential applications and its role in advancing digital transformation. We are committed to providing extensive in-kind support, including access to our facilities, resources, and expertise, to ensure the successful development and implementation of the project. We believe that their participation will not only enrich the project but will also bolster IU’s mission to contribute to impactful research and foster global academic collaboration.
We look forward to the possibility of collaborating on this exciting project and are committed to providing all necessary support to ensure its successful completion.
Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require any further information or clarification.
Sincerely,


Assoc. Prof. Dinh Duc Anh Vu
Vice-rector
International University, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City
