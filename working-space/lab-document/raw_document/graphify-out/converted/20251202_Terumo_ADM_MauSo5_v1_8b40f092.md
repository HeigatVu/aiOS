ACCEPTANCE LETTER
Dec 03rd, 2025
To whom it may concern:
We are pleased to confirm that International University - VNU HCM has officially approved the hosting and management of the research project titled:
“Early Detection of Alzheimer’s Disease and Mild Cognitive Impairment in Vietnamese Older Adults through Speech Analysis”
Principal Investigator (PI): Dr. Ha Thi Thanh Huong
Funding Source: Terumo Life Science Foundation’s R&D Subsidies (Overseas) Program
Funding Organization: Temuro Life Science Foundation
The project is approved for implementation at our institution and will be conducted from January 2026 to March 2027. We acknowledge and agree to comply with the grant’s requirements.
We fully support this project and will facilitate its implementation in accordance with our institutional policies and the guidelines set by the funding organization.
Please let me know if you require any additional information.
Sincerely,


Assoc. Prof. Dr. Le Van Thang
President
International University, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City
