# Plan — DSD/SSD Speech Data Protocols Literature Review

**Slug:** `dsd-ssd-speech-data-protocols`
**Created:** 2026-05-16
**Status:** in-progress

## Scope & Key Questions

1. **Data Collection Protocols** — What standardized assessment batteries, elicitation tasks, and spontaneous speech sampling methods are used for children with DSD/SSD? Focus on protocols designed for or used in clinical settings.

2. **Recording Environments & Methodologies** — What microphone setups, noise conditions, sampling rates, and equipment are typical? Which datasets record in clinics vs. homes vs. schools? What about telepractice/remote recording?

3. **Clinical Datasets** — What publicly available or research-accessible speech corpora exist for children with DSD/SSD? Target datasets with annotations (phonetic transcriptions, severity labels, diagnosis labels).

4. **Speech Therapy Protocols** — What intervention protocols (e.g., cycles approach, minimal pairs, complexity approach, core vocabulary, PROMPT) involve structured speech data that could be modeled? How are exercises personalized?

5. **Relevance to Multimodal Models / LLMs** — Which data characteristics matter for simulating child speech or personalizing therapy exercises? Phonetic features, error patterns, articulatory data, visual (facial/mouth) data.

## Source Types

- **Papers** (primary): Speech-language pathology journals, Interspeech, ICASSP, JSLHR, AJSLP, Clinical Linguistics & Phonetics
- **Web/Docs**: Clinical guidelines (ASHA, RCSLT), dataset documentation, tool documentation
- **Repos**: GitHub repositories for speech assessment tools, clinical datasets, therapy apps
- **Time period**: 2010–2026, with emphasis on 2018+ for recording methodology and multimodal/LLM relevance
