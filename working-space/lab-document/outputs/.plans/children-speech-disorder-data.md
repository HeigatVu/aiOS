# Plan: children-speech-disorder-data

**Topic:** Review data collection protocols, assessment tasks, and speech recording methodologies for children with developmental speech disorders (DSD) and speech sound disorders (SSD), focusing on clinical datasets, recording environments, and speech therapy protocols/tasks for collecting data to train multimodal models or LLMs.

**Date:** 2026-05-16

## Key Questions

1. **Q1:** What publicly available or clinically documented speech datasets exist for children with DSD/SSD? (corpus size, age range, languages, annotation types)
2. **Q2:** What speech recording protocols and environments are documented? (microphone setups, noise control, clinical vs. home vs. lab settings)
3. **Q3:** What assessment and therapy tasks are used for data collection? (picture naming, repetition, spontaneous speech, phonological probes)
4. **Q4:** How are these datasets annotated? (phonetic transcription, severity labels, intelligibility scores, clinical classifications)
5. **Q5:** What existing work combines these datasets with multimodal models or LLMs for child speech simulation or exercise personalization?
6. **Q6:** What are the consent, privacy, and ethical considerations unique to pediatric speech data?
